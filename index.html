Privacy Policy & Terms

At Givano, we respect and value your privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal information and handling your data transparently.

  • Information We Collect: We collect necessary account details (such as your name and email address) to enable secure sign-in and facilitate item discovery.
  • Data Usage: Your data is used solely to provide, operate, and maintain the Givano platform and ensure user trust and security.
  • Data Sharing & Third Parties: We do not sell or trade your personal information to third parties or data brokers.
  • User Safety & Communications: You retain ownership of the content you submit. You can request account or data deletion at any time.

For full legal terms, questions, or data deletion inquiries, please reach out directly to our support team at contact@givano.com.
